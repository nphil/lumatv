# Auto brightness: what the curve is based on

Code: `helper/app/src/autobright.rs` (all parameters are the constants at its top, each with its reason). Tests with a fake clock and a fake sensor: `autobright/tests.rs`.

## Sources

- Android, `AutomaticBrightnessController` ([source](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/master/services/core/java/com/android/server/display/AutomaticBrightnessController.java)): the sensor is filtered by a short and a long time-weighted mean; the accepted light moves only when it leaves a hysteresis band and has stayed out of it for a debounce; the screen then fades to the new level. Defaults in the framework config: 4 s to brighten, 8 s to darken ([config.xml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aosp-mirror/platform_frameworks_base/main/core/res/res/values/config.xml), `config_autoBrightnessBrighteningLightDebounce` 4000, `config_autoBrightnessDarkeningLightDebounce` 8000). Darkening is the slower side everywhere.
- Room light at a TV, CLASP/CLTC field study of 60 homes, 7 days, a meter at the bezel ([report](https://www1.eere.energy.gov/buildings/appliance_standards/pdfs/analysis_of_background_illuminance_levels_during_television_viewing.pdf)): most viewing is between 0 and 100 lux, very little at 0 lux (the screen's own light), night rooms sit at steady levels for hours and change in steps when a lamp is switched, daytime rooms drift over hours with the sun and windows. The study recommends test points between 10 and 100 lux, not 0 and 300 as the ENERGY STAR ABC procedure had ([ENERGY STAR TV v8.0](https://www.energystar.gov/sites/default/files/Final%20V8.0%20TVs%20Program%20Requirements_0.pdf)). The set's sensor reads lower than a meter in the room (1 to 3 lux dark, 6 to 8 with a lamp, measured), so the curve's lit end is 30 lux and its dark end 1.
- Perceptual steps: lightness follows luminance roughly logarithmically (Weber–Fechner), and the smallest visible change is about 1 to 2% of the current luminance for a slow change ([Stanford EE368, point operations](https://web.stanford.edu/class/ee368/Handouts/Lectures/2019_Winter/2-Point_Operations.pdf); [Poynton, luminance, brightness and lightness for HDR](https://library.imaging.org/admin/apis/public/api/ist/website/downloadArticle/cic/30/1/6)). The panel's setting moves in whole units, 2% of a level of 50, so a single step sits at the edge of visibility and what hides it is the time between steps. [INFERENCE: the 1 to 2% figure is from the literature, not measured on this panel; it still wants an eye check on the set.]
- wluma ([repo](https://github.com/max-baz/wluma)) learns a curve from the owner's own corrections and screen content as well as the sensor. The owner's correction (a slider move sets the level wanted in that light) is already how this curve learns. Screen content (average picture level) is the `Decide` input `content`, empty for now: the screen's own light reaching the sensor is handled by the bands, the longer darkening debounce and a sudden shift's size, not by compensating for it.

## The curve in plain terms

- The level is a log curve of the room's light from half the owner's level at 1 lux or less up to the owner's level at 30 lux or more; the owner's level is never exceeded, and a slider move fits the curve through it.
- The sensor is read every 2 s. The room's light counts as changed only when the 6 s and the 30 s means both leave a band (+30% or -40%, at least 2 lux) and have stayed out of it for 10 s (brighter) or 30 s (darker). Flutter inside the band, a blip from the screen, a shadow: nothing.
- A drift (daylight, a dimmer) is followed one unit at a time, at most 0.8% of the level a second up and 0.4% down (a unit every 2.5 s up and 5 s down at level 50), and starts only for a target 2 or more units away, so it neither oscillates nor chases rounding.
- A sudden shift (a lamp on or off: at least double and 4 lux away for two readings in a row going up, three going down) is accepted at once and followed by an eased ramp: up to 10% of the level a second up and 6% down at its fastest, slowest at both ends, a 24-unit change is over within 12 s of the lamp going on and 20 s of its going off (asserted by the tests), always in single units.
- Switching it on ramps from the picture's value. Only a picture mode coming into force (a video starting) goes to its level in one write, hidden by the mode change.
