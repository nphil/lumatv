## Answer in short

1. **The sub window is the app's to ask for.** Inside every media process LG's AV connector (`libav-connector.so.1`, the `avc.*` log lines) chooses the video sink: `MainVideo` (2) by default, `SubVideo` (4) when the load's option `avSink.videoSink.type` is `sub_video` (`BIN: libplayerAPIs parseLoadMessage`, `libav-connector _setSink`, `convertSinkType`: `main_video`, `sub_video`, `graphic`, `main_sound`, `sub_sound`, `mixing_sound`). A `SubVideo` connector acquires `SUB_SCALER` (plus `MAIN_SOUND` with audio) and asks `videooutputd` to connect the decoder to `SUB0`; `videooutputd` sets the window mode `pip` by itself when the client names none (`BIN: videooutputd doConnect`, "set winMode(pip) automatically"). That is exactly what YouTube's pipeline did in LG's Multi View (`TV: luna-lgmv-both-playing`), only there the connector decided from the Multi View controller's state instead of the option.
2. **What LumaTV must provide is foreground standing, nothing else.** `videooutputd` connects a video only for an app on SAM's foreground list and the media server marks a pipeline foreground from the same list, so the compositor lists the pipeline's app (Home) beside the card in front while the claim holds the sub window. The entry is plain (no Multi View marks): marked entries make the controller count itself launched with `mode` `full`, which the connector reads as `view type 0`, ends its Multi View state and goes back to the main sink, ignoring the app's own choice ("Multiview Mode : ignore sinkInfo from App"). NDL cannot take the sub window at all: its load is a low-delay game session, and the pipeline then acquires `SUB_SCALER` for itself (`StarfishMediaAPIs::acquireSubScalerResource`, the `subScalerOccupied` videooutputd reported), so its connector's `MAIN_SCALER` request runs into the app's video and fails (`TV: e2c-messages.txt`, error 601 `Scaler Resource Allocation Failed`, then the connector asks the controller to close a Multi View that was not one).
3. **Not depending on any of that, a camera can play over any app today**: when no hardware window can be had, the pop-up and the picture-in-picture are granted a **texture**: the feeder decodes the camera's low stream with FFmpeg's decoders (loaded from `lib/` beside it, LGPL) and paints its own window; the compositor draws that window stretched over the claim's rectangle, under the shell's glass. The order is automatic: hardware window (main, then sub) -> texture -> stills. The full view never takes a texture.
4. **Limits.** The sub window decodes at most 1920x1088 at 30 (VP9 60) and exists only while no 4K120 video holds both scalers; the texture is 1280x720 at 30 at most (the CPU's), drawn in the UI's pixels (the 1920x1080 page the display scales), at the cost of a decoder on the CPU while it plays.

## What is established

1. **An app cannot choose the sub window.** `PUB: NDL_directmedia_types.h`: `NDL_DIRECTMEDIA_DATA_INFO_T.video` is width, height, codec and one unknown int. On webOS 4 an app said `AcbAPI_setSinkType(SINK_TYPE_SUB)`; on webOS 5+ that sequence is gone (`PUB: ss4s smp_resource_webos5.c` stubs it; the window id in the load is all the app hands over).
2. **The policy** (`SRC: umediaserver ResourceManager::selectPolicyCandidates`): when a pipeline cannot get what it asks for, the pipelines that hold it are ranked by (type priority, foreground, focus, visible, last use) and the lowest ones that hold what is needed are told to release it (`released by policy action`, error 600); the requester and everything ranked above it are never touched. The set's table adds a ladder above that: `levels` = overlay, screen_saver, **multiview**, card, floating, then background (`TV: gfn/tv-2026-10-26/umediaserver_resource_config.txt`): a Multi View app's pipelines outrank a card app's.
3. **Which sink a video gets** (`SRC: umediaserver ConnectionPolicy.cpp, VideooutputConnector.cpp`): `try_connect` returns -1 unless the pipeline is **foreground** (its app is on SAM's foreground list *and* the pipeline said `notifyForeground`); then it takes the sink the resource manager acquired for it, else the first sink that is free or whose video is in the background, else a sink whose video has no focus. Round 20's finding that `videooutputd` connects a video only for an app id on SAM's foreground list is this rule.
4. **The foreground list is where Multi View shows.** LG's compositor publishes, for each fullscreen app, `multiview`, `pip`, `primary`, `viewType` (`mvsxs` or `mvpip`) and `order`, and in Multi View lists both apps (`SRC: uiinv/qml/qml/WebOSCompositor/services/base/StarfishForegroundAppInfoMgr.qml`); SAM republishes it as `foregroundApps` and the media server parses it (`TV: compositor/slot-2026-10-28-r15/gfn-browser/B-lumatv/gfn-lumatv-msgs.txt`: `fgAppsEvt: {... "foregroundApps":[{"order":0,"pip":false,"multiview":false,"primary":true,"viewType":"normal",...}]}`). The Multi View controller reads the same list (`mainAppId`, `subAppId`, `viewType`, `is4KHFRSignal`: the same file's `MVC` lines).
5. **The sub window's limits are the codec file's** (`TV: gfn/tv-2026-10-26/device_codec_capability_config.json`, `multiview_sub`): 1920x1088 at 30 for H.264, H.265 and AV1, VP9 at 60; the main window 4096x2304 at 60 (H.265 120). The kernel docs say "Sub scaler is responsible for outputting video to a sub window in the Multi View mode" (`SRC: video-texture.rst`). A 4K120 video occupies both scalers (`V4L2_CID_EXT_VSC_OCCUPATION_SUB_SCALER`, `SRC: v4l2-ext-vsc.h:1938-2011`), and then there is no sub window for anyone (`is4KHFRSignal`).
6. **LumaTV's NDL session asks for more than a camera needs.** `videooutputd` read the feeder's session as `lowDelayMode true`, `isGameMode true`, `subScalerOccupied true` on `MAIN` at 1280x720 and 30, with `hfrEnabled false` (`TV: compositor/slot-2026-10-29-r20/hwplanes/camprobe-home.txt`): the flag is not the kernel's rule for sources over 2K at high frame rates (point 5), so what sets it for a low-delay session is open (P1). With the policy of point 2 that fits both failures of round 20 [INFERENCE]: a second NDL pipeline asks for what the first holds and, being newer, takes it (error 600 on the first); the foreground app's own id gives the feeder the same rank as the app, and the later one wins MAIN.
7. **Multi View is started and owned by the Multi View controller (MVC), and nothing in the calls LG's own UI makes names a sub app.** The quick panel's tile calls `multiviewcontroller/launchMode {"mode":"sxs"|"pip","usageType":"normal"|"dual_monitor"}` (`SRC: uiinv/qml/qml/QuickSettings/Controllers/MultiViewInterfaces`, `Constants.js`); the compositor and key filters call `setInputMode {appId,inputMode}`, `closeApp {appId}` (empty id: end Multi View) and `changeControlMode {role}`, and subscribe to `getStatus`, `getMultiviewStatus`, `getMediaControlStatus`; the rest is audio (`selectAudioOut`, `setMixAudio`, the mix volume calls) and `setAppCoordinate` (`SRC: uiinv/qml/qml/WebOSCompositor/views`, `KeyFilters/systemUi.js`, `appLaunch.js`). SAM lets only `com.webos.service.multiviewcontroller` launch an app with the reason `multiview` (`TV: census1/dc-census/x/etc/configd/layers/base/com.webos.applicationManager.json`, `launchReason`); the picker that chooses the second app is LG's own overlay app (`com.webos.app.multiviewsettings`), which is not in the inventory. The kernel's video scaler doc has the same shape (`SRC: scaler.rst`, Multi View): "two apps perform (A) connect the input resource to the main scaler ... (A-2) connect the input resource to the sub scaler (e.g. the VDEC input source), then the window settings"; PIP and PBP are one control (`V4L2_CID_EXT_VSC_WIN_PROP`), "the sub window can support any input", "even if the main video path is disconnected, the sub video path can still work": the driver knows sources and windows, not apps. The device layers of the O22 family set `supportMultiview` and `supportDualMonitor` true (`TV: configd/layers/devicename/o22*/multiview.json`).

## What is not established

Neither is in OSE (its resource manager has no `levels`). Nothing in the repo or in the set's journal has an active Multi View session in it. P1 and P3 settle it.
- **Whether the NDL session's both scalers come from NDL's low-delay load or from its declared ceiling** (on webOS 10.3.1 a pipeline allocates against what the load declares, from another project's lab report on a K24 set: an H.264 load declaring 4K60 refused, the same envelope declared as H.265 accepted). The feeder cannot change either through NDL; a Starfish-API feeder could, and is not built (it needs LG's C++ ABI and a toolchain this tree has no sysroot for).

## The other mechanisms, read

- **GAV graphic rendering (the four graphic scalers): LG's own answer to "two or more hardware-decoded videos at once".** `SRC: gp-scaler.rst`: "GAV ... a unified compositor that integrates video rendering and graphic rendering ... Previously, only up to two media could be played due to hardware limitations. By supporting graphic rendering, two or more HW decoded videos can now be played simultaneously ... used for remote meetings, hbbtv ads, YouTube dual video test cases". Path: VDEC -> VDO (`/dev/video27`, `V4L2_CID_EXT_VDO_VDEC_CONNECTING`) -> GPSC (`/dev/video70`: `S_INPUT` the VDEC instance, `S_FMT` the size, at most 1920x1080 NV12 or ARGB, `REQBUFS`, `EXPBUF`, `QBUF`, `STREAMON`, `DQBUF`) -> dma-buf to the compositor -> a GPU texture; "more than four instances"; the set's table has `GP_SCALER 4`, `VTG 1`, pipeline types `vtg` and `vcs`. The connection from a decoder to the GPSC is made by LG's GAV pipeline (umediaserver acquires, LSM composites, `videooutputd` connects); no NDL or Starfish option for it is known. **Usable in principle without DRM bypass** (our own decoder's frames), **not built**: the decoder is LG's pipeline, which this feeder does not own. The texture claim is backend-agnostic: a feeder that painted its window from a GPSC buffer would need no compositor change.
- **V4L2 stateful decoder (`/dev/video21`, "lgsoc-vdec-decoder"), the node LG's web engine uses to decode into memory** (`SRC: core/vdec/ipc/local/v4l2_vdec.c`, `vb2_hma.c`, `vb2_cpb.c`, `media-vda-vea.rst`). Read for the mmap hazard that wedged `/dev/video24` (`venc-result.md`): the capture queue's memory is `vb2_hma` and its mmap is `remap_pfn_range(vma, ..., buf->dma_addr >> PAGE_SHIFT, ...)` of a `hma_alloc_user` block after an exact size check, the bitstream queue's `vb2_cpb` the same shape; there is no `page_address(vmalloc_to_page(phys))`. **The mmap is not the hazard here. The decoder is:** a client first acquires the hardware from the media server and hands the result to the driver (`V4L2_CID_EXT_RES_INFO`), so a decoder opened without that is one the resource manager does not count, and an app that then cannot get a VDEC (it has four) loses its picture. Not adopted, and no probe that opens the node is proposed.
- **The video tap (`/dev/video60`, `libVTG`, `VT_CAPTURE 1`) and `/dev/vo` raw frames** copy or feed the display path; neither decodes a second stream (`display-chip-direct.md` §4.5, `venc-result.md`).
- **CPU decode: built** (below). Four A76 cores at 1.4 GHz are the budget (`venc-result.md`); a camera's second stream is a small part of one.

## What was built

- **Feeder** (`gfn/feeder`): `GF_TEXTURE` (width, height) before a load makes the load a texture session: `gf_swdec.c` loads `libavutil-lumatv.so.N` and `libavcodec-lumatv.so.N` from `lib/` beside the program (H.264 and H.265 decoders only, one thread, low delay, no loop filter on pictures nothing refers to, checked against the major it was built for), `gf_yuv.c` halves the picture by 2x2 boxes while it stays above the target and samples it bilinearly into XRGB, `vpl_wl.c` gained `vpl_wl_resize` and the window's two buffers are painted in turn (a buffer the compositor still holds is not painted: the picture is a counted skip). The process runs at nice 5 while a texture plays, refuses a stream over 1920x1088 or 60 a second at the load, and says in the load's answer why it cannot (libraries missing, too large, a format that is not 8-bit 4:2:0). No NDL and **no LS2 role** is needed for it. One stats line a second (`"tex"`: pictures, skipped, errors, decode and convert microseconds mean and worst, source and output size, the process's CPU %). Build: `gfn/feeder/third_party/build-ffmpeg.sh` (FFmpeg 9.0.2, sha pinned, LGPL, shared, the two decoders, ARM soft-float with NEON, host x86-64), `build.sh` copies the soname files to `build/{arm,host}/lib/`; the libraries are 1.9 MB and 0.8 MB; the feeder is not linked to them (the ARM binary needs only libc, libpthread, libdl, as before).
- **Compositor** (`vwin.rs`, `scene.rs`, `window.rs`): `Decision::Texture`; a camera-popup or pip claim that every window refused (`app-video`, `both-scalers`, `no-window`, `too-big`) is granted `slot:"texture"` (`texture:true`, limits 1280x720 at 30, at most two at once); never `camera-full`, never in standby or with `ctl vwin off`; it holds no window, so the app's video, both scalers and the windows' switch do not end it, standby, `off` and `ctl vwin texture off` do. The claim binds to the feeder's video window by app id as before; `placed` and `sink:"texture"` come once the window has painted at a size of its own; the scene draws that window's buffer stretched over the claim's rectangle (a `Pose` at rest, the transitions' own path) in the video layer, under the shell. `ctl vwin texture on|off`, the topic's `texture`, the journal line `vwin: claim X (kind) granted a texture (no window: REASON)`.
- **Helper** (`live`, `scrypted/routes.rs`): `window:"texture"` of `POST /v1/cameras/live` reaches the feeder as `GF_TEXTURE` with the stream's own size scaled to fit 640x360 (the compositor does the rest); no NDL app is sent for it.
- **Packaging**: `feeder.sh` stages `lib/` beside the program (install and update; remove takes it away; `status` has `texture`), `build-ipk.sh` carries it. A texture needs the feeder staged (`lumatv feeder install`, the owner's command, as for the window path); it does not need the role.
- **The sub window** (SubWindow): the feeder's pipeline is a driver behind one table (`gfn/feeder/src/gf_pipe.h`): `gf_ndl.c` as before, and `gf_smp.c`, the starfish pipeline (`libplayerAPIs.so.1`, the class `StarfishMediaAPIs` called by its mangled names, the ES load of webosbrew/samples `media/smp` with the stream's own size as the decoder's ceiling and no game mode). `--pipe ndl|smp` and `GF_PIPE`, `--sink main|sub_video` and `GF_SINK` choose them before a load; NDL refuses a sink; `--delay-load MS` leaves the window up before the load for a probe's claim. The compositor's `Vwin::fg` (Home, plain, on by default; `ctl vwin fg APP [mv]|off`) lists the app on `getForegroundAppInfo` from the moment a claim is granted the sub window (`luna_methods.rs` `sub_foreground`). The helper loads a `window:"sub"` picture in the starfish pipeline with the sub sink, for Home, 150 ms after the grant (`live::load`, `SUB_LIST_SETTLE`).
- **Tests**: `tests/test_gfn_feeder.py` (Yuv, Texture: the picture against FFmpeg's decode and area scale at 22 dB or better, the engine protocol end to end with a resize while loaded, a unit that is no picture, the end of the session, the missing libraries, the size guard, H.265), `gfn/feeder/tests/yuv_test.c` (colours of both matrices and both ranges, flat, ramp, checkerboard, odd sizes, refusals, under ASan and UBSan), `gfn/feeder/tests/arm_decode_check.sh` (the ARM build of the decoders, NEON on, against the host's, picture by picture under `qemu-arm-static`: 120 H.264 pictures at 720p and 28 H.265 at 360p give the same checksums; the static musl twin of the shipped build, since the shipped one is glibc and needs the set's loader), `tests/test_feeder_install.py`, `compositor/.../vwin.rs` (the allocator: textures for every refusal reason, never the full view, few, ended by standby and by their switch) and `tests/vwin.rs` (the texture drawn over the claim's rectangle and nowhere else, moved with the claim, ended by `texture off`, no video placed; the foreground list with and without the experiment), `helper` (`texture_size`).

## Costs

Measured on the host with `gfn/feeder/tests/swdec_bench.c` (CPU time of the thread, so a loaded machine does not inflate it; an i9-9900K at 3.6 GHz, FFmpeg's decoders without x86 assembly, a test pattern at about 3 Mbit/s, which is harder than a camera): 1280x720 into 480x270, **3.7 ms to decode + 2.0 ms to convert = 5.7 ms a picture**; into 960x540 3.8 + 4.7 = 8.6 ms; 640x360 into 480x270 1.3 + 1.2 = 2.5 ms. The set's four A76 cores at 1.4 GHz running AArch32 with NEON are not this machine: [INFERENCE] two to three times the convert and about the same decode, so a 640x360 stream at 15 a second should take under 10 % of a core and a 1280x720 stream at 30 a second 30 to 45 %. The feeder's own `tex` line and `swdec_bench` on the set (T0) say which. The converter is plain C (the cross compiler here does not vectorize it for NEON, checked with `-S`; its output is fixed to the bit by golden checksums in `yuv_test.c`, so a faster one can replace it); if T0's convert time is the larger half, FFmpeg's libswscale (NEON assembly for the scale and the conversion, one more shared library of the same build) is the replacement, behind the same `gf_yuv_to_xrgb` call. Memory: the feeder's window buffers are two of the picture's size (640x360x4 = 0.9 MB each), the compositor uploads one a picture (0.9 MB at 30 a second is 28 MB/s), the decoder's frames are a few MB. No GPU work beyond one textured quad over the claim's rectangle, damaged only where it changes.

## Changelog line

Camera pictures play over any app: where no video window can carry one (an app's video, a 4K120 game, no window free) the pop-up and the picture-in-picture are granted a texture, decoded by the feeder on the CPU and drawn by the compositor over the claim's rectangle; the foreground list of LG's Multi View is an experiment switch (`ctl vwin fg`).

A camera plays in the display's second hardware window beside an app's video: the feeder loads it in LG's own starfish pipeline asking for the sub sink (the option every app may set), and the compositor lists the pipeline's app on the foreground list while the claim holds that window; no CPU decode, the texture stays the fallback.
