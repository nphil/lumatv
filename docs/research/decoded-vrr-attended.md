# Internal 4K120 VRR: attended qualification

**Decision: no timing experiment yet.** The stock display controller has separate pixel and timing selections. There is no demonstrated path from a completed internal 4K picture to the next transmitted frame. That does not prove a display firmware change is necessary, or that one would suffice. The current LumaGame feeder submits compressed data; it cannot return a retained decoded picture or its release cookie. The [host presenter and paired test](decoded-vrr-4k.md) do not change the retail backend.

### 1. Rehearse recovery with stock software only

Approval: one attended stock restart, separately from any instrumentation or timing change. No experimental writes. Before shutdown, record the boot ID, boot-image digest, loader options, lack of firmware overrides and working stock picture, sound and Home handback. Use the normal shutdown path and allow OLED maintenance to finish. If its state is uncertain, do not disconnect power.

After power-up, require a **fresh normal load**, not just a new boot ID: the C4 can [resume a snapshot](boot-direct.md). Compare the three [embedded image identities](rea-firmware-result.md) and the read-only `/proc/lg/de/fw-status` against baseline. In the boot log, distinguish `normal` from resume/restore/skip, the header selection for each index (2, 1, 0) from a RAM backup, and final controller readiness from proof of all three copies. In `core/de/vsc_fw.c:400–424,527–546,586–601`, each iteration overwrites the return value; only index 0 reaches the final per-index `done` message. Do not demand nonexistent success messages for 2 and 1, or count the summary as verification of them. Missing independent evidence for either image leaves recovery **functionally demonstrated at most**, not a verified restoration of all bytes. Do not write the firmware-history control to manufacture a receipt.

Repeat stock 50/60-family and 4K120 playback, check sound and picture, exit to Home and confirm normal ownership. Stop on any mismatch, lost link, missing provenance or failed handback. A verified physical cold boot could recover volatile code independently of a wedged MCU; it cannot undo panel damage or restore a live session. A reboot during a later trial is a failed handback, never normal cleanup or an automatic action.

### 2. Qualify the observer without changing output timing

**New candidate: `CRC_TOUT`.** The public O22 driver assigns a dedicated hardware interrupt, distinct from `CRC_VOUT0/1` (`chip/o22/os/linux_irqs.h:163–171`; `chip/o22/slt/slt_o22.h:100–103`). The handler reads the pixel checksum and stores host monotonic microseconds **on every second handled interrupt**, up to 150 samples; it acknowledges status. The timestamp is taken in software, not latched at the wire (`slt_o22.c:110–163,203–221`; `include/slt_kapi.h:70–76`; `core/base/os_util.c:580–586`). Even with one interrupt per scan, alternating 10/16 ms and fixed 13 ms both give 26 ms between saved timestamps. Opening the factory device enables checksum hardware; it is not a passive read. The O22 webOS TV build disables the driver (`platform/o22_webos_udtv/platform_dev.mk:3`; `core/modules.mk:31,72`). Retail `de_proc_slt_status` prints queued strings, not these samples (`0xffffffc010924830`; `core/de/de_proc_slt.c:97–125`). The MCU's `[crc0]`/`[crc1]` dump compares two memory buffers (`0x502ca4f0–0x502ca526`, `0x502ca951–0x502ca9bb`); it is not a receiver acknowledgement.

Approval: witness-only, after identifying an actual retail owner and the tap's position relative to the V-by-One transmitter. Prefer a receiver frame-start record; otherwise require a documented transmitted boundary with calibrated phase, **including repeated pictures**. A controller reporting its own timing request is not independent. With stock fixed 120 and 60 Hz output, capture every event, a monotonic time bound and the tagged picture identity. Hold one dim picture for several scans; then change picture IDs at 60 pictures/s on 120 Hz output. Require two events per picture in the latter control, one per repeat in the former, and no unexplained gaps, overwrites or variable timestamp delay that obscures a 2 ms shift. Known rates alone do not establish where the tap sits. Stop if any requirement fails. No factory diagnostic, new driver or blind polling is part of this plan. A transmitter witness proves digital delivery only: the panel may retime the picture after receipt.

### 3. Attempt one off-grid frame, not a mode change

Approval: separate, attended, for one specific reversible timing change. Preconditions: stages 1 and 2 pass; a real backend holds a tagged 4K picture until explicit retirement; and an owner can arm one bounded wait and return to stock even if the app dies. Otherwise no run. No flash, boot override, ForceVRR, change in panel protections or increase in brightness.

At stock 4K120 SDR, record the unmodified control. For one trial, keep the pixel source on the decoded picture path. Arm a single wait before the 8.33 ms boundary; release it only when the retained picture becomes eligible, at +11 ms from the previous transmitted frame. The timing producer must not know +11 in advance. Require a stock repeat by +16.67 ms if release fails, enforced independently of the experiment. Record actual eligibility, reference edge, tracker wait exit, every transmitted boundary and ID, and final retirement. A matching ID on an off-grid boundary with no intervening repeat is the first positive result. A fixed tick, an unmatched ID or absent boundary is not. Abort on the first unexpected boundary, lock loss, hang or abnormal handback; restore through the stock owner and verify state. No automatic reboot.

One positive trial warrants the [paired discriminator](../../game/vrr-probe/paired_evidence.py), **not** a VRR switch: +11/+13 ms, unchanged-reference and held-picture controls, full timing uncertainty within the 0.25 ms tolerance. Product qualification additionally requires the usable range, repeats below it, audio sync and stock handback after exit, cancellation and process death. Neither 144 Hz nor a lower VRR limit follows from a 4K120 result.
