## AR24 pages by default, and the video check (PageAlpha, 2026-10-29)

XR24 pages made every video hole opaque black (`fb0` `global_alpha_en 1`): a video app played sound and showed no picture. `ctl pagealpha on` made YouTube visible on the TV (round 19, `compositor/slot-2026-10-29-r19w` `img/r19w-yt-display-pagealpha-on.jpg`), so the pages are AR24 from the start: `Config::page_alpha` defaults to true, `--page-alpha off` and `ctl pagealpha off` are the opt-outs (refused with `--vwin` or `--afbc on`).

- **Fallback.** A ring that cannot be made as AR24 (CMA short, a plane that does not list AR24) is made as XR24 and the compositor starts: the log says `VIDEO HOLES ARE OPAQUE BLACK` as an error, `ctl status` has `pageFormat` and `videoHolesOpaque` (true), `ctl afbc` `pages.videoHolesOpaque` and `pages.fallback` the reason. A runtime `pagealpha on` that finds no memory keeps the old pages and says so the same way.
- **Alpha of the page, audited.** Every pass blends premultiplied (`ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`) over an opaque clear: the page is opaque black before the windows (the engine's stack 0 clears to alpha 1, the defined-texture rule makes new pages alpha 1), a hole is cleared to alpha 0 and nothing that is drawn lowers an opaque pixel's alpha (the shaders scale colour only: dim, box dim, the pose; backdrops and glass blend over the opaque page). Over a hole a translucent layer stays translucent, so the video shows through it. The grab for screenshots and recordings still forces alpha 255; `Kms::capture` (frame dumps) reads the page as it is. Tests: `tests/page_alpha.rs` (hole alpha 0, translucent strip over the hole and over opaque pixels, an XRGB client buffer's meaningless alpha byte), `afbc.rs` (the fallback chain), `config.rs` (the default and the opt-out), `tests/vwin.rs`.
- **Work under a video.** With a full-screen video app and nothing over it the compositor draws one frame (the page is the hole) and nothing after: 120 ticks compose 0 frames and flip 0 pages (`tests/page_alpha.rs`). A layer over it (a toast, a volume bar) composes only its own tiles: **1.2 % of the page** (25,600 px, 0.10 MB written at 4 bytes a pixel) against 100 % (2.09 Mpx, 8.4 MB) for a whole page, one frame per commit of its animation, and the first frame after it goes gives the rectangle back to the video and then nothing again. This needed a fix in `Scene::plan`: any change in the list of windows used to damage every window's rectangle, so a toast over a full-screen video owed the whole page; now only the windows that came, went, moved or changed owe theirs (a restack with the same windows, a `redraw_all` and a changed hole list still owe all they covered). Scan-out reads 497.7 MB/s at 60 Hz for XR24 and AR24 alike (both 4 bytes a pixel); the AR24 page costs the GPU nothing more per pixel (the shaders already wrote alpha), `[INFERENCE]` for the display engine's blend, which the `gate.sh video` numbers below settle. The host's GL is Mesa software: its microseconds are relative only.
- **What the set did under a video (round 19, YouTube, `compositor/slot-2026-10-29-r19w/video/oj*`).** Nothing over the video still composed 1 to 2.5 frames a second (30 s: 76 UI frames of 77 draws and 56 layers, 3.1 Mpx owed, 12 ms to the GPU's finish each): the Rust Home went on ticking its analogue clock's second hand and its news slide behind the app, and the engine draws stack 0 under the window. The same Home was drawn under every overlay frame (79 draws, 59 layers). The shell client now stops Home's timers and updates while an app covers it (`shell.rs`, `home/model.rs`, `home/surface.rs`), and the compositor does not draw it while an app's card covers it and nothing moves (`Plan::home_hidden`, `Gl::set_home_hidden`: the owed rectangles are cleared to black, the card and the system layer go over them, and the frame that finds Home uncovered draws it whole; `tests/transitions_rust_home.rs`). The overlays' own flips are 16.7 ms apart; `ctl refresh` `intervals` count the video's idle gaps and are no stall measure; `motion` is (flips over 20 ms after the frame of an animation that was still running: the engine's demand at every refresh, or a transition). A GPU kept on (`power_policy always_on`, `power/control on`) changed nothing.

## What was found

### The holes (the finding that decides the rest)

`TV: docs/research/arch/scanout-hw-02.txt` (LumaTV's compositor holding the DRM master): `fb0/status` reads `hdr.global_alpha_en: 1`, `hdr.global_alpha: 0xff`; under LG's compositor (AB24 pages) the same field reads 0 (`scanout-live-01.txt`, `framerate/*/before.txt`). `SRC: core/fbdev/fbdev_drm.c osd_drm_flip_image`: "setup global alpha if pixel format is xRGB": for XR24 the driver turns the constant alpha on, so the OSD ignores every pixel's alpha. The compositor's primary pages are XR24 (`kms/mod.rs make_page(.., alpha=false)`), and a video hole is a clear to transparent black (`gl.rs clear_holes_before`): on the panel that is **opaque black**. The recorded symptoms fit: the VIDEO capture is the clip (round 15/16 wallpaper, correlation 0.998) while LG's DISPLAY capture is black in every hole (`slot-2026-10-28-r15/img/wallexp-v1-display.jpg` against `wallexp-v1-video.jpg`), and the Netflix DISPLAY capture of 2026-10-26 is black at the centre (mean 0.0) while the picture mode says it plays. The panel itself was never looked at. `--page-alpha on` makes the pages AR24 (the plane lists it, `drmplanes-01.txt`): the frame already holds correct premultiplied alpha. Whether the OSD blends premultiplied is check P2 below.

**Confirmed on the panel (2026-10-29 18:45, Round19W, `compositor/slot-2026-10-29-r19w/log.txt`):** with XR24 pages YouTube played full screen with sound and a black picture under LumaTV's compositor (`global_alpha_en 1`); `ctl pagealpha on` converted the ring to AR24 (`global_alpha_en 0`, CMA free 4.8 MB before, 5.9 after) and Nitin saw the video at once. P0 passes as the note above expected; the OSD blends the frame's alpha as AR24 (no dark fringes were reported on Settings' glass over the video). LG's DISPLAY capture is the blend of video and UI (it showed the YouTube picture afterwards, `img/r19w-yt-display-pagealpha-on.jpg`) and was black in every hole before. Not a regression: XR24 has been the composed ring's only format since the KMS output (`bfd0965`).

### Video windows

- Two windows, not four. `SRC: chip/o22/de/de_reg_o22.c:140` `NUM_WIN_MAX 2`; `win2`/`win3` exist as proc nodes and enum values, `DE_REG_O22_CviMap` gives win1 and win2 one clock select and win3 none. `TV:` `videooutputd`'s status lists exactly two sinks, `MAIN` (z 0) and `SUB0` (z 1) (`arch/x4/all.jsonl`); across the seven app runs of `framerate/` (Netflix 4K24, Plex 1080p24, YouTube 1080p and 4K, 24 to 60 fps) `MAIN` is connected and `SUB0` never is, `subScalerOccupied` is `false`.
- The one exception: LumaTV's own NDL session. `TV: round8/tv-2026-10-25/log.txt:392` (the saver, 1856x1024 at 60, low-delay and game mode on): `sink MAIN`, `subScalerOccupied: true`. If every NDL session does that, the sub window is never free next to LumaTV's own video. Check V2 reads it for the wallpaper's session.
- The sub window is small. `TV: gfn/tv-2026-10-26/device_codec_capability_config.json`: `multiview_sub` is 1920x1088 at 30 (VP9 at 60) for H.264, H.265, AV1; the main window is 4096x2304 at 60 (H.265 at 120). A camera's 4K main stream needs the main window.
- A 4K120 video takes both scalers (`framerate-result.md`: `V4L2_CID_EXT_VSC_OCCUPATION_SUB_SCALER`, `subScalerOccupied`). Resource table: one main scaler, one sub scaler, three display managers (`umediaserver_resource_config.txt`).
- Z-order: below every OSD pixel; between windows `videooutputd` orders by `zOrder` (main 0, sub 1: the sub window is over the main one). The compositor's `setCompositing` exists (`luna::video::set_compositing`) and is sent when a client sets the element's z index.
- Scaling limits: none in the source (`limited_win_ratio_*` always read 0, `v4l2_vsc_event.c:3143`). Unmeasured; check V6.
- The OSD mixer has three rectangular hardware holes (`osd_reg_o22.h` `reg_hole0..2`, x/y/w/h/alpha/reverse, per-input select): LG's drivers never program them. TV 2026-10-29 (`layer-deep-result.md` § 8.4): with AR24 pages, hole 0 on B.OSD1 (`0xCC4802C8`, `0xCC4802CC`, hole register `0xCC48029C` = 3) opens the video plane through Settings' glass at the commanded place, `x` in 1920-wide UI units and `y`, `w`, `h` in panel pixels; select value 1 opens nothing. It is a hardware window where page alpha is not wanted or the page cannot carry it (an opaque panel and XR24 pages with the right geometry are not retested). Not built into the compositor: a register write needs the owner's approval, and page alpha already does the same job per pixel.

### The cursor plane

`SRC: core/drm/drm.c`, `core/fbdev/fbdev_drm.c`, `chip/o22/fbdev/fbdev_hw_o22_cmn.c:2895-3050`:

- Plane 36 (`crsr_fb`, OSD3), AR24 only, at most 128x128. Positions are `unsigned` in the UI's 1920x1080 space (`osd_drm_set_cursor_region` follows the primary plane's size) and are scaled to the panel (x2); the image is scaled x2 as well, by LG's blitter, bilinear, only when the image's framebuffer changes (`detect_cursor_change` compares the struct, not the pixels).
- The hotspot is the framebuffer's `hot_x/hot_y`, which only `DRM_IOCTL_MODE_CURSOR2` sets; the display driver subtracts it and clips at the top and left edge ("H/W doesn't accept negative position"). An atomic plane update has no hotspot and a negative `crtc_x` becomes a huge unsigned value: that route clamps at the edge.
- The legacy cursor ioctls take the kernel's unsynchronised path (`drm_atomic_helper_update_plane` sets `legacy_cursor_update` for the CRTC's cursor plane, `drm_atomic_helper.c:2886`): no vblank wait, no `-EBUSY` against the flip in flight. An atomic commit through the `ATOMIC` ioctl does wait/EBUSY. Hence the legacy route first, on a thread of its own (the commit's `hw_done` still waits for the in-flight frame's fence).
- The buffer's width: `lg1k_drm_gem_fb_create` aligns pitches to 64 bytes and the legacy ioctl names the pitch as `width * 4`: 32 pixels (pitch 128).
- `TV: scanout-hw-02.txt`: `fb3/status` is `crsr_fb`, `visible: off`, 0 flips; `fbdev` module parameters `fbdev_cursor_x_comp`, `fbdev_cursor_y_comp`, `fbdev_cursor_ignore_fbmem_update` exist on the installed module. The installed driver is dated 2021-02-15, older than the source.

### AFBC pages

- The plane lists `0x0800000000000062` (AFBC 32x8 split sparse) for AB24 and XB24 (`TV: drmplanes-01.txt`); `lg1k_get_format_info` knows it for those two formats only; the OSD decodes one AFBC layout (the kernel passes "AFBC on", not the flags).
- LG's compositor scans such buffers out: AB24, 1920x1080, pitch 7680, 8,486,912 bytes, and a 768x2160 secondary of 6,742,016 (`TV: scanout-research.md` E3). The size is `align4096(superblocks * (16 + 1024))` with the height taken up to 1088 rows: 60 x 136 x 1040 = 8,486,400 -> 8,486,912, and 24 x 270 x 1040 -> 6,742,016, both exact (`afbc::buffer_size`, tested). Sparse layout: the memory a page takes does not shrink, only the bytes read and written.
- Not known: whether the 64-bit g24p0 blob draws into an AFBC image. `EGL_EXT_image_dma_buf_import_modifiers` is listed on both blobs (`arch/x2/x2-run-02-importers.txt`); `ctl afbc` / `--afbc probe` read `eglQueryDmaBufModifiersEXT` (check A1). The 32-bit r46p0 blob draws them (it made LG's buffers).
- CMA: three linear 1080p pages are 24.9 MB of a free CMA of 3.5 to 12 MB at the reads on record. A whole new ring beside the old cannot be had, so a live switch converts one page at a time (`ctl afbc on|off`, `ctl pagealpha on|off`); it needs one page (8.3 MB) free and refuses with the reason when it is not. Check A3 reads CmaFree first.
